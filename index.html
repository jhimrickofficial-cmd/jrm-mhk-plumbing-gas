FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question regarding your plumbing or gas setup? Here are straightforward answers.

What services does MHK Plumbing & Gas offer?

We specialize in complete residential and light commercial plumbing solutions, including leak detection, tapware repairs, sewer and stormwater drain unblocking with CCTV cameras, hot water cylinder installations, appliance connections, and certified gasfitting.

What are your standard operating hours?

Our trade team operates weekdays Monday through Friday from 7:30am to 5:30pm. We are closed on weekends, ensuring our master tradesmen are fresh and dedicated during regular project hours.

Are your plumbers and gasfitters licensed and insured?

Yes. MHK Plumbing & Gas Pty Ltd holds full Australian master trade accreditations, gas certification licenses, and comprehensive public liability insurance coverage for maximum peace of mind.

Do you provide compliance certificates for gas installations?

Yes. Following any gas line installation, appliance hook-up, or repair, we provide an official Certificate of Compliance certifying that the system meets Australian Gas Safety Standards AS/NZS 5601.

How do you structure your pricing?

We provide upfront, transparent quote estimates before starting work. You will always know what to expect with zero hidden charges or unpleasant surprises upon job completion.
